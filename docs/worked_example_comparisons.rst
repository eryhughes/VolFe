===================================================================================
Comparison to other degassing models
===================================================================================

There are a variety of degassing tools including CHOS-bearing species available and picking which one is best for your application can be tricky! 

Check out `Iacovino et al. (pre-print) <https://doi.org/10.31223/X59B79>`_ for a comparison of D-Compress (`Burgisser et al. 2015 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cageo.2015.03.002>`_), EVo (Liggins et al. `2020 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsl.2020.116546>`_; `2022 <https://doi.org/10.1029/2021je007123>`_), MAGEC (`Sun & Lee 2022 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.gca.2022.09.044>`_; `Sun & Yao 2024 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.epsl.2024.118742>`_), Sulfur_X (`Ding et al. 2023 <https://doi.org/10.1029/2022gc010552>`_), VolFe, and the S-free VESIcal (`Iacovino et al. 2021 <https://doi.org/10.1029/2020ea001584>`_).

Additionally, for the `'Modelling the behaviour of volatiles in magmas' <https://kaylai.github.io/GS2026-volatiles-workshop/>`_ workshop associated with Goldschmidt in Montréal, Canada, we produced some `Jupyter notebooks <https://github.com/eryhughes/workshop_volatiles_goldschmidt2026>`_ to compare calculations of:
1. Pressure of vapor saturation between VESIcal and VolFe, and
2. Degassing paths between VESIcal, Sulfur_X, EVo, and VolFe.
